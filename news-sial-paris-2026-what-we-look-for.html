SIAL Paris 2026: The products we are looking for

SIAL Paris runs from 17 to 21 October. Here is what kind of products we want to meet there, and what to bring to a first conversation.

SIAL Paris takes place from 17 to 21 October 2026 at Paris Nord Villepinte. I will be there to meet producers and brands that want to enter the German food retail market.

Who we are

Project P Distribution is a Berlin-based importer and distributor. We bring food and beverage products that are not yet available in Germany to German retail, with a focus on EDEKA. We choose products with market data, not gut feeling: before we recommend a product, we look at what is already on the shelf, at price points and at what German shoppers respond to.

What we are looking for

  • Genuinely new for Germany. Products you cannot yet buy in German supermarkets, or only in a very different form.
  • A clear point of difference. Taste, ingredients, format or story. If a shopper cannot see the difference in three seconds, it is hard to win the shelf.
  • A format that works in German retail. Sensible pack sizes, a good shelf life and a price point that fits the category.
  • Export readiness. Stable supply, product data, certificates and a team that answers quickly.

Categories we follow most closely: snacking and delicatessen, non-alcoholic beverages, bakery and confectionery, nuts and dried fruit, oils, spices and preserves.

What to bring to a first meeting

  1. A product sample, if possible.
  2. A specification sheet with ingredients and allergens.
  3. Your price list (ex-works) and minimum order quantities.
  4. Shelf life, pack and pallet data, and EAN codes.
  5. Your food safety certificates.

You do not need everything on day one. A short conversation is enough to find out whether there is a fit.

How to reach us

Use the contact form on our website and mention "SIAL", or message me on LinkedIn. We cannot take every product, and we will tell you early if a product is not the right fit for the German market. That saves both sides time.

Assad Abou-Chaker, Founder & Business Development, Project P Distribution GmbH
